# Law 01 — Reflection outline

- Follow Nia, a transit maintenance technician, through two accounts of a shop incident. Give the apprentice an independent explanation and a concrete interest in what happens next.
- Separate observation, inference, and memory. Examine how prior mistreatment can teach a useful protective habit that carries costs in a new setting.
- Treat reflection as both personal and institutional. Use the Bertrand and Mullainathan field experiment to show how judgments can be tested. Keep the study's scope tied to its method.
- Move from identifying a mistake to repairing its effects. Distinguish a private apology from correcting a public record.
- Prepare the transition to Phone Down by ending with the attention needed to hear evidence that would otherwise go unnoticed.
