# Law 07

Compare material and nonmaterial inheritance, document discriminatory assessment, and teach financial basics.

The print expansion connects six topics: regional circulation and family wealth, reciprocal accountability in commerce, household banking and credit, neighborhood change, local civic records, and knowledge and care as intergenerational assets. Law 07 carries the principal discussion of dependable fatherhood and male mentorship, with related sections in Laws 06 and 13.
