# THE 14 LAWS TO GROOVE

## Lessons for a Stronger Tomorrow

What do we owe ourselves, our communities, and the people who come after us?

In *The 14 Laws to Groove*, Jabari Jaja brings fourteen practical principles to decisions about work, money, relationships, and community. Rooted in Black American life, the book considers how personal choices and institutional decisions shape what people can do.

The laws ask readers to examine a judgment before acting on it, keep promises others depend on, understand the terms of an opportunity, and share knowledge that can make it easier for somebody else to find their way. Each law opens and closes with questions and includes a lined reflection page.

**Know who you are. Understand where you stand. Give purpose to where you're going.**
